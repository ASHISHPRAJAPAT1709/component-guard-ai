Model & Methodology Specifications

ISRO RESEARCH ARCHITECTURE

Mathematical formulation and explainability framework underpinning the ComponentGuard AI prototype.

MODULE A

Dynamic Anomaly Detection

  • Lot / Peer Comparison: Computes the dynamically normalized baseline $\mu_{lot}(t)$ and median deviation across all active dies in the same production wafer/lot.
  • Robust Deviation Analysis: Flags components exceeding peer statistical envelopes even if they remain strictly within absolute datasheet limits ($\text{Value} \le 50.0\,\mu\text{A}$).
  • Prototype Anomaly Score:
    $\text{Score} = \min\left(100, \frac{|x_i(t) - \mu_{lot}(t)|}{\mu_{lot}(t)} \times 25\right)$
    Yields 0–39 (Normal), 40–69 (Warning), 70–100 (Anomalous).
MODULE B

Predictive Drift Analysis

  • Historical Time-Series: Evaluates burn-in parameter samples at $t \in \{0\text{h}, 24\text{h}, 96\text{h}\}$ to calculate velocity $dx/dt$.
  • Deterministic Extrapolation: Evaluates rate of change across thermal stress states (125°C) to predict $t=168\text{h}$ value before completing the full burn-in cycle.
  • Safety Threshold Comparison: Compares predicted 168h value against safe operational boundary ($35.0\,\mu\text{A}$), enabling 72-hour early screening termination.
EXPLAINABILITY

Human-in-the-Loop Decision Support

  • Peer Deviation Factor: Quantifies exact percentage divergence relative to sister components.
  • Drift Trend Acceleration: Evaluates whether parametric degradation is accelerating or plateauing.
  • Risk Reasoning: Synthesizes multiple physical failure modes into understandable plain-text engineering rationales.
  • Decision Sovereignty: Preserves final screening authority for qualified QA personnel.
